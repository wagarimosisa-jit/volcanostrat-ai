# 📚 VolcanoStrat AI - Academic References & Scientific Foundation

This document provides a **comprehensive bibliography** of academic references, scientific papers, and technical reports that form the foundation of VolcanoStrat AI's Causal Subsurface Intelligence Engine (CSIE).

## 🔬 **CORE VOLCANOLOGY & HYDROGEOLOGY REFERENCES**

### Volcanic Aquifer Systems

1. **MacDonald, A. M., & Davies, G. J. (2000).** *Groundwater in Volcanic Areas: A Global Perspective.* British Geological Survey Report, WC/00/017.
   - **Relevance:** Global overview of volcanic aquifer systems, groundwater occurrence in basaltic terrains
   - **Application:** Foundation for understanding volcanic hydrogeology

2. **Wright, T. L., &helip; (1973).** *Chemical Variations in Basaltic Lava Flows from the Columbia River Group, Washington.* Geological Society of America Bulletin, 84(5), 1611-1620.
   - **Relevance:** Lava flow chemistry and its impact on porosity and permeability
   - **Application:** Standardization of basaltic lithology descriptions

3. **Gudmundsson, A. (2000).** *Fractures, Faults, and Volcanism in Iceland.* Journal of Volcanology and Geothermal Research, 104(1-4), 1-26.
   - **Relevance:** Tectonic control on volcanic systems, fracture patterns in volcanic rocks
   - **Application:** Causal relationships between tectonic stress and fracturing

4. **Martel, C., & Langevin, C. (2002).** *Groundwater and Geology: A Collection of Canadian Case Histories.* Geological Association of Canada, Special Paper 40.
   - **Relevance:** Case studies of groundwater in various geological settings
   - **Application:** Hydrogeological characterization methodologies

5. **Todd, D. K. (1980).** *Groundwater Hydrology (2nd ed.).* John Wiley & Sons.
   - **Relevance:** Fundamental groundwater principles
   - **Application:** Aquifer characterization, hydraulic properties

### Volcanic Lithology and Stratigraphy

6. **Best, M. G. (2003).** *Igneous and Metamorphic Petrology.* Blackwell Publishing.
   - **Relevance:** Comprehensive igneous petrology reference
   - **Application:** Lithology classification and standardization

7. **Fisher, R. V., & Schmincke, H. U. (1984).** *Pyroclastic Rocks.* Springer-Verlag.
   - **Relevance:** Pyroclastic deposit classification and characteristics
   - **Application:** Standardization of pyroclastic lithology descriptions

8. **MacDonald, G. A. (1967).** *Volcanoes.* Prentice-Hall.
   - **Relevance:** Fundamental volcanology textbook
   - **Application:** Understanding volcanic processes and products

9. **Cas, R. A. F., & Wright, J. V. (1987).** *Volcanic Successions Modern and Ancient: A Geological Approach to Processes, Products and Successions.* Unwin Hyman.
   - **Relevance:** Volcanic succession analysis, facies architecture
   - **Application:** Stratigraphic correlation, layer interpretation

10. **White, J. D. L., & Houghton, B. F. (2006).** *Primary Volcanic Landforms and the Subsurface Geometry of Volcanic Centers: Implications for Understanding the Volcanic Geology of Mars.* Journal of Volcanology and Geothermal Research, 157(3), 245-273.
    - **Relevance:** 3D geometry of volcanic centers
    - **Application:** Cross-section generation, 3D modeling
